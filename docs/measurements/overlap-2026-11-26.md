# Overlapping voices on the 54 s shop clip, 2026-11-26 (Mac M2 Pro)

Question: are the enrolled person's missed words (19.7, 20.1, 28.1 s) and the wrong ones (12.6 s another voice,
24.7 s nothing said) caused by voices that overlap, and does separating them first fix the count?
Task, clip, enrolment (CAM++, 120 s of another recording) and the owner's truth (10 words, times at word end,
tolerance 0.6 s) are those of [testbed-2026-11-26.md](testbed-2026-11-26.md). Threshold 0.35 everywhere; it was
chosen from this clip earlier. Nothing here is a held-out accuracy.

## What ran

- **PixIT** (`pyannote/speech-separation-ami-1.0`, pyannote.audio 3.3.2, its published config, CPU): 8 s blocks with
  4 s of earlier sound; each word scored with CAM++ on every separated source active at it (1.5 s around the word),
  best score kept. Offline on `a.wav`, and once inside the recipe (`--with overlap`).
- **SepFormer** (`speechbrain/sepformer-whamr16k`, two sources, 8 s windows with 2 s more on each side), offline only.
- **pyannoteAI** hosted (`precision-2`): one voiceprint from 30 s of the enrolment audio, one identification of the
  whole clip, inside the recipe (`--with hosted`). Its turns are evidence beside each word, not the decision.

## What this says

- Separation changes the scores, but not reliably: moving PixIT's blocks by 10 ms moved word scores by up to
  0.18 (27.44 s: 0.224 -> 0.405; 22.30 s: 0.421 -> 0.352). PixIT itself is deterministic on CPU (same input, same
  output, checked twice). The recipe run gave other PixIT scores than the offline run on the same words (the cause was
  not isolated; its blocks start where the video's sound starts). The 7-of-9 result above is
  within this spread, so PixIT is **not** turned on by default.
- pyannoteAI's diarization put the person's words from 11 to 23 s into the same speaker as the other person who
  talks from 48.5 s, and matched the voiceprint only to the person's later speech (24-48.6 s, confidence 89-90 of
  100). An excited voice is a second cluster for that model too. This points at the voice changing, not only at
  overlap.
- 24.7 s ("nothing said") is an ASR error. A voice check cannot remove a word the ASR invented while the person
  is speaking (pyannoteAI: the enrolled voice is speaking at 24.4-25.3 s).
- SepFormer (trained on English synthetic mixtures) lowered the person's scores more than the others'.
