# Two test-bed clips, 2026-11-26 (Mac, CPU, small models)

Both clips were also used to choose settings. Nothing here is a held-out accuracy.

## Slams in a 11.9 s, 60 fps clip with cuts and zooms

Reference: 15, by an AI agent from 20 fps frame sheets looked at before GAMST ran (the number was written down after its first output);
15 impact sounds lie at the same moments. No person counted it.
A second bout (9.3-10.8 s: rocking and arm movements without an impact) was not counted as slams.

| setting | counted | right | extra |
|---|---|---|---|
| text rule, first try (wrist above shoulder, then 0.8 shoulder widths below within 0.4 s) | 18 | 15 | 3 (arms lowered, no impact) |
| + speed condition (new `rate` measure), threshold 10-18 | 16 | 15 | 1 |
| + speed, threshold 20 | 15 | 15 | 0, but only in a narrow range |
| first rule + impact sound (`scene --with impact`: `audio-onsets` + `coincidence`, 30 ms before to 150 ms after) | 15 | 15 | 0 |

The onset sensitivity was the module's default (3); 4 and 5 give the same 15. The 30/150 ms window was chosen on this clip.

### Slams that hit something without a sound (owner, 2026-11-26)

The owner defined a slam as a collision, heard or not: 2.02 s (both fists from the head onto the armrests, the chair
shakes, no impact sound) counts. Reference now 16.

| setting | counted | right (by end time, 0.2 s) |
|---|---|---|
| text rule: wrist above shoulder, falls faster than 10 shoulder widths/s, then stops (slower than 3/s) below the shoulder within 0.15 s; sound only shown beside each count | 16 | 16, mean time error 9 ms |

The same 16 for fall thresholds 8, 10 and 12 and stop thresholds 3 and 5. 15 of the 16 have an impact sound 43-69 ms later.

## One word by one person in a 54 s clip of several people in a shop

There is no certain reference: nobody listened. From voice scores and lips: 7 utterances by the person are clear
(11.6, 12.5, 22.0, 28.0, 37.7, 46.2, 47.5 s), 2 likely by the lips (19.4, 19.7 s), about 4 undecided (20.1-20.4, 21.0, 24.0 s).

| setting | counted |
|---|---|
| before: 3 s windows, gate before ASR, Whisper small | 0 (`--without voice`: 2 of anybody) |
| after, Whisper small, speaker threshold 0.35 | 7 |
| after, Whisper large-v3-turbo, speaker threshold 0.35 | 8: 6 of the 7 clear ones + 21.0 + 24.0; misses 28.0 (voice score 0.27), 19.4 and 19.7 (0.18, 0.24) |

At the default threshold (0.5) only utterances above 0.5 count. 0.35 was chosen from the gap seen in this clip
(most of the person's words 0.40-0.57, others mostly below 0.28). The voice was enrolled from another recording of the person (120 s).

What was wrong and is fixed: a file given to `--kind voice` used only its first 1.5 s; fixed windows cut words; one verdict
covered a window shared by several people (now each word is scored with 1.5 s around it); the gate muted words before they were read.

Not fixed: an excited voice scores low; a word said off camera by a voice the model cannot place stays undecided. Lips
(active-speaker detection) decided two such cases by eye and are not a GAMST module.

### The owner's corrections (2026-11-26, by listening)

The person said the word at 12.0, 14, 19.7, 20.1, 21.5, 22.3, 28.1, 38.2, 46.6 and 47.7 s (10; times as shown when a word ends).
12.6 s was another voice; 20.3 s another voice; at 24.7 s the word was not said (ASR error); from 49 s another person repeats it.

Turbo at threshold 0.35 against these 10: counted 8, right 6, precision 0.75, recall 0.6.
Missed 14.0 (not transcribed), 19.7 and 20.1 (voice scores 0.18 and 0.24), 28.1 (0.27); wrong 12.6 (0.42, another voice), 24.7 (not said).
A larger speaker model (WeSpeaker w2v-BERT 2.0, VoxCeleb weights, 2.3 GB + 2.3 GB backbone, Mac CPU) did not separate them
either: 12.6 s (another voice) scored 0.52-0.53, 19.6 s (the person) 0.23-0.35, with 1-2 s of sound around each word.
Overlapping voices are the likely cause, not the size of the model.

The voice embedding alone does not separate these people on this audio; faces and lips are the next evidence (not built).
Separating overlapping voices first (PixIT, SepFormer) and hosted identification (pyannoteAI) did not count reliably
better: [overlap-2026-11-26.md](overlap-2026-11-26.md).

Speed (recorded input, whole recipe): small 34-45 s, turbo 90-117 s for 54 s of sound: turbo is not realtime on this CPU.
The slam recipe took 28.5 s for the 11.9 s, 60 fps clip (pose 31 ms per frame, every frame processed): not realtime at 60 fps.
