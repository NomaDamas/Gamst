# Two test-bed clips, 2026-11-26 (Mac, CPU, small models)

Both clips were also used to choose settings. Nothing here is a held-out accuracy.

## Slams in a 11.9 s, 60 fps clip with cuts and zooms

Reference: 15, by an AI agent from 20 fps frame sheets looked at before GAMST ran (the number was written down after its first output);
15 impact sounds lie at the same moments. No person counted it.
A second bout (9.3-10.8 s: rocking and arm movements without an impact) was not counted as slams.

| setting | counted | right | extra |
|---|---|---|---|
| text rule, first try (wrist above shoulder, then 0.8 shoulder widths below within 0.4 s) | 18 | 15 | 3 (arms lowered, no impact) |
| + speed condition (new `rate` measure), threshold 10-18 | 16 | 15 | 1 |
| + speed, threshold 20 | 15 | 15 | 0, but only in a narrow range |
| first rule + impact sound (`scene --with impact`: `audio-onsets` + `coincidence`, 30 ms before to 150 ms after) | 15 | 15 | 0 |

The onset sensitivity was the module's default (3); 4 and 5 give the same 15. The 30/150 ms window was chosen on this clip.

## One word by one person in a 54 s clip of several people in a shop

There is no certain reference: nobody listened. From voice scores and lips: 7 utterances by the person are clear
(11.6, 12.5, 22.0, 28.0, 37.7, 46.2, 47.5 s), 2 likely by the lips (19.4, 19.7 s), about 4 undecided (20.1-20.4, 21.0, 24.0 s).

| setting | counted |
|---|---|
| before: 3 s windows, gate before ASR, Whisper small | 0 (`--without voice`: 2 of anybody) |
| after, Whisper small, speaker threshold 0.35 | 7 |
| after, Whisper large-v3-turbo, speaker threshold 0.35 | 8: 6 of the 7 clear ones + 21.0 + 24.0; misses 28.0 (voice score 0.27), 19.4 and 19.7 (0.18, 0.24) |

At the default threshold (0.5) only utterances above 0.5 count. 0.35 was chosen from the gap seen in this clip
(most of the person's words 0.40-0.57, others mostly below 0.28). The voice was enrolled from another recording of the person (120 s).

What was wrong and is fixed: a file given to `--kind voice` used only its first 1.5 s; fixed windows cut words; one verdict
covered a window shared by several people (now each word is scored with 1.5 s around it); the gate muted words before they were read.

Not fixed: an excited voice scores low; a word said off camera by a voice the model cannot place stays undecided. Lips
(active-speaker detection) decided two such cases by eye and are not a GAMST module.

### The owner's corrections (2026-11-26, by listening)

The person said the word at 12.0, 14, 19.7, 20.1, 21.5, 22.3, 28.1, 38.2, 46.6 and 47.7 s (10; times as shown when a word ends).
12.6 s was another voice; 20.3 s another voice; at 24.7 s the word was not said (ASR error); from 49 s another person repeats it.

Turbo at threshold 0.35 against these 10: counted 8, right 6, precision 0.75, recall 0.6.
Missed 14.0 (not transcribed), 19.7 and 20.1 (voice scores 0.18 and 0.24), 28.1 (0.27); wrong 12.6 (0.42, another voice), 24.7 (not said).
A larger speaker model (WeSpeaker w2v-BERT 2.0, VoxCeleb weights, 2.3 GB + 2.3 GB backbone, Mac CPU) did not separate them
either: 12.6 s (another voice) scored 0.52-0.53, 19.6 s (the person) 0.23-0.35, with 1-2 s of sound around each word.
Overlapping voices are the likely cause, not the size of the model.

The voice embedding alone does not separate these people on this audio; faces and lips are the next evidence (not built).
Separating overlapping voices first (PixIT, SepFormer) and hosted identification (pyannoteAI) did not count reliably
better: [overlap-2026-11-26.md](overlap-2026-11-26.md).

Speed (recorded input, whole recipe): small 34-45 s, turbo 90-117 s for 54 s of sound: turbo is not realtime on this CPU.
The slam recipe took 28.5 s for the 11.9 s, 60 fps clip (pose 31 ms per frame, every frame processed): not realtime at 60 fps.

## Slams in real time (2026-11-27, Mac, Apple silicon, 32 GB)

The slam clip again (`testbed/b.mp4`, 11.9 s, 60 fps, 714 frames), text rule `hit_10_3.json`, truth `truth_hit.json`
(16 slams, matched one to one by end time within 0.2 s). Runs: `try-it/testbed/slam/rt.sh` (reports, logs and
`NAME.score.json` beside it, scored by `score.py`), one at a time under `guard.sh`. **Latency** is the word-to-HUD
equivalent: the capture time of the slam's end (the event's `end_ms`) to the moment the Store knew the count (L1: when it
was committed; final: when its revision, or the added count, was committed); the HUD draws what the Store holds.
**Memory** is the peak of the summed RSS of the run's process tree, sampled every 0.5 s; the GPU processes' Metal and
shared-memory pages count in each process, so it overstates (a CPU-only run: 574 MB). Nothing here is a held-out accuracy:
one clip, also used to choose.

Before (coordinator, 2026-11-27): `--as-live` with MediaPipe full on the CPU counted 14 of 16 and lost 190 frames in 10
gaps ("lane pose needs 33 ms per frame, camera gives one every 17 ms"); every frame processed: 16/16, 28.5 s.

### Few-shot examples (coordinator, 2026-11-27, `try-it/testbed/slam/fewshot.sh`)

3 slams cut from the same clip (b.mp4@2.72-2.99, 3.02-3.29, 3.32-3.59) and 1 negative (9.3-10.8 s, rocking without
impact); thresholds enter 0.169 / exit 0.254 (`leave_one_out_and_negatives`). Offline: 15 found; of the 13 held-out
slams 12 right, 0 false. Missed: 2.05 s, the silent both-fists slam onto the armrests (a different motion from the
examples). 41 s wall for the 11.9 s clip.

### Where the time went (per frame, 1280x720)

| step | ms |
|---|---|
| decoding (OpenCV, in the Pipeline's event loop) | 0.7-1.4 |
| MediaPipe full, CPU, inference only (4 poses) | 33-35 |
| MediaPipe full, GPU delegate (Metal), inference only | 6-10 |
| sending a frame to the pose worker through the pipe | about 8 |
| ... through shared memory (new) | under 1 (round trip on the GPU 18.4 -> 10.5) |
| GPU input as a reversed copy + RGBA conversion, then one `cvtColor` (new) | 8 -> 0.7 |
| RTMW-l 384x288, CoreML (ML Program, CPU+GPU): pose / YOLOX-m detector | 29 / 27 |
| ... ONNX Runtime CPU: pose / detector | 211 / 377 |
| ... CoreML on the Neural Engine: pose | 154 |
| ... CoreML GPU, 8 crops per call: per crop | 15.2 (1 crop: 21.8) |

Decoding was not the problem (not moved off the event loop). MediaPipe's GPU delegate works on macOS
(mediapipe 0.10.35) with 4-channel input only; with 3 channels it aborts (`unsupported ImageFrame format`).

RTMW: the usable state of the art per `docs/research/speaker-pose-sota-2026-11/report.md`. RTMW-x 384x288 (COCO-WholeBody
70.2 AP) has no published ONNX file; the run uses RTMW-l 384x288 (70.1 AP, 17.7 GFLOPs; rtmlib's `performance` whole-body
model, `rtmw-dw-x-l_simcc-cocktail14_270e-384x288`), sha256 `bd033156...b83054`. CPU and CoreML gave the same keypoints on the
frames compared (ML Program, float32); a full CPU run was not made (590 ms per frame). The few-shot exemplar for RTMW was
enrolled with RTMW from the same examples (`rt_fs_rtmw.json`, enter 0.121 / exit 0.181). **Sapiens2** (accuracy #1 in the
report) was not downloaded or run: its licence forbids biometric processing; it needs the owner's licence decision.

On this clip RTMW is not more accurate than MediaPipe full at 60 fps (equal by the rule, one fewer by few-shot), but it keeps
16/16 at 30 fps where MediaPipe full drops to 13/16: MediaPipe loses fast falls when it sees fewer frames.

### Live: L1 counts at once, a verification lane settles (`--with verification`)

L1 = MediaPipe full on the GPU (`--set pose.delegate=gpu`) in a `sample="newest"` lane (the recipe default now): while busy
it keeps only the newest frame, so it skips evenly and the skip is a declared choice. Verification = RTMW-l on CoreML over
**every** frame (`backlog="spill"`, video memory: 256 MB in memory, then a spill file), detector every 500 ms; its
counts settle L1's (`final`, `revoked`, `uncertain`, or added). `lost` is 0 in every run below.

### Real-time factor (processing time / frame interval; below 1 keeps up)

| lane | ms per frame (live, shared GPU) | 60 fps | 30 fps |
|---|---|---|---|
| MediaPipe full, GPU (L1) | 9-18 | 0.5-1.1 | 0.3-0.6 |
| MediaPipe full, CPU | 30-45 | 1.8-2.7 | 0.9-1.4 |
| RTMW-l CoreML, detector every 500 ms (verifier) | 29-37 (59 in one run) | 1.7-2.2 | 0.9-1.2 |
| RTMW-l CoreML, detector every frame | 65-85 | 3.9-5.1 | 2.0-2.6 |
| RTMW-l, Neural Engine | 276 | 16.5 | 8.3 |
| RTMW-l, CPU (benchmark, pose + detector) | about 590 | 35 | 18 |

At 60 fps the verifier falls behind by about 0.8 s per second of video: on this clip it finishes 7-14 s after the slam;
on a long stream its spill file grows until the 8 GB bound (about 48 s of 60 fps 720p), after which frames are declared
lost. What would make the SOTA lane itself keep up, not built: batching crops (8 per call: 15.2 instead of 21.8 ms per crop
on CoreML), running the detector less often or on the Neural Engine while the pose model has the GPU, a CUDA GPU (not
measured here), or a 30 fps camera (measured: final latency 1-3 s, nearly keeping up).

**Default now:** `pose` samples the newest frame (live runs skip evenly; recordings that are not paced still get every
frame). Recommended on this Mac: `--set pose.delegate=gpu --with verification` (verifier RTMW-l, detector every 500 ms).
The GPU delegate stays opt-in: it is measured on macOS only.
