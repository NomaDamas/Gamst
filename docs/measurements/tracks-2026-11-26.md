# Speaker tracks on the 54 s shop clip, 2026-11-26 (Mac CPU, paced as live)

One clip, several people, the same clip as [testbed-2026-11-26.md](testbed-2026-11-26.md). Nothing here is a
held-out accuracy, and no setting was chosen on this clip: threshold 0.5 (module default), pieces 2 s, stable 8 s,
settle 10 s. The Nemotron mode (`low_latency`) was chosen for speed only (below).

Run: `gamst recipe bench person-term-count --with tracks --as-live` (default), Nemotron-3 diarization (CPU), CAM++.
Words were replayed (`transcript-replay`) at the moment their ASR gave them:

- **truth times**: the owner's 15 labelled moments, each word 0.35 s long, final 0.3 s after its end (assumed);
- **Scribe VAD**: ElevenLabs Scribe v2 Realtime, server VAD commits (one long utterance, finals about 23 s late);
- **Scribe endpointer**: the same with GAMST's client endpointer (median 2.7 s word -> final; 14.0 s not transcribed).

## What decided it

Nemotron's slots (low_latency) do not follow people on this clip. The person's moments fall in all three slots:
slot 0 holds the person at 19.74, 20.14, 28.1 and other voices at 12.64, 20.34, 27.44, 50.4; slot 1 the person at
14.0, 21.46, 22.3 and other voices at 49.9 and 52.2; slot 2 the person at 38.2, 46.64, 47.74 and another voice at
20.74. A slot's identity is one score for all its words, so a slot that mixes people is right for some of them and
wrong for the others whatever the threshold. Scores of each slot over the run (owner's voice): slot 0 0.70 -> 0.81
-> 0.69, slot 1 0.26 -> 0.47 -> 0.51, slot 2 0.36 -> 0.59.

Leakage: slot 0's first piece holds the owner's reference sound (11.02-12.52 s), so with that voice slot 0 scores
0.67-0.81; with the other recording 0.39-0.43. Every word of slot 0 is affected, not only 12.04 and 12.64.

The word nobody said (24.70 s; Scribe wrote it at 24.16) had no active slot and was not counted in any tracks run.
The word snippet check misses most words when finals are late: its sound ring holds 30 s, and the server-VAD
utterance becomes final 34 s after its first word.

Latency: with truth times (ASR final assumed 0.3 s after the word) a word is provisional 1.3 s after its end
(Nemotron low_latency gives activity in 720 ms steps with 320 ms look-ahead, plus a 200 ms margin). With Scribe it is
the ASR's own delay. Settling takes 6.5-10 s (p50), bounded at 60 s.

Nemotron-3 alone on this CPU (real-time factor, 54 s): `ultra_low_latency` 0.87, `very_low_latency` 0.48,
`low_latency` 0.25; slots were similar in all three. CAM++ costs about 17 ms per second of sound.
