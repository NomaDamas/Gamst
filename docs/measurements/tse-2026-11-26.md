# Target speaker extraction on overlapped speech, live, on the 54 s shop clip (2026-11-26, Mac M2 Pro)

Design: [../realtime-tse.md](../realtime-tse.md). One clip (`a.wav`, several people in a shop), paced as live
(`--as-live`), so nothing here is a held-out accuracy. Voice: `voice.json` (CAM++, 120 s of another recording of the
person); the TSE model is conditioned on that recording's audio (`--voice-wav e2s.wav`). Owner's truth: the person's
word ends 12.04, 14.0, 19.74, 20.14, 21.46, 22.30, 28.10, 38.20, 46.64, 47.74; others 12.64, 20.34, 20.74, 27.44,
49-52 s; nothing said 23.8-24.2. Overlapped cases (owner): 12.04/12.64, 19.74-20.74, 27.44/28.10 (8 words).

## 2. Live overlap detection (Nemotron-3 slots, low_latency, live)

`MultiVoice` on the run's `audio.speaker_activity` records (`scripts/qa_tse.py`); the 8 overlapped words count as
covered when a stretch reaches within 0.2 s of their end.

| detector | stretches | flagged (of 54.3 s) | overlapped words covered | person's non-overlapped words flagged |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| near (2 slots within 1.5 s), the default | 9 | 21.8 s | **7/8** (all but 12.04) | 14.0, 21.46, 47.74 |
| overlap (2 slots in the same 10 ms) | 8 | 6.9 s | 3/8 (19.74, 27.44, 28.10) | 14.0 |
| Precision-3 (offline reference), near | | 30 s (0.56 of the clip) | 8/8 | 14.0, 21.46 |
| Sortformer v2.1 (slower than real time here), near | | 15 s | 5/8 | none |

The Nemotron records were the same in every run of this note (deterministic on CPU). Both rules were computed on
this clip before `near` was kept as the default; `near` follows from the per-word score's own 1.5 s context, which is
the failure the owner's decision addresses (voices within a second of each other), not from these numbers.

## 3. Per overlapped word (run v2: `--with tse --with verification`)

Heard = the TSE stretch's recogniser (a Scribe v3 Live fork with the same key term) wrote the term in the extracted
sound within 1 s of the word; score = CAM++ on 1.5 s of the extracted sound around it (window = the whole extracted
span); decision at the module threshold 0.5 (not chosen on this clip).

| truth end | who (owner) | stretch (detected)? | heard in extracted (end / score) | window score | TSE decision | right? | final (L4) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12.04 | person | no (stretch starts 12.31) | - | - | (room score 0.35: not counted at once) | - | counted (L4) |
| 12.64 | other | yes | **yes** 12.63 / 0.41 | 0.51 | uncertain | not counted: right, but the word leaked into the extraction | not (`uncertain_overlap`) |
| 19.74 | person | yes | yes 19.89 / 0.27 | 0.35 | uncertain | not counted: missed (said so) | not (`not_heard`) |
| 20.14 | person | yes | yes 20.09 / 0.34 | 0.35 | uncertain | not counted: missed (said so) | not (`not_heard`) |
| 20.34 | other | yes | (not written by L1) | 0.35 | - | - | - |
| 20.74 | other | yes | **yes** 20.71 / 0.29 | 0.35 | uncertain | not counted: right, but leaked | not (`not_heard`) |
| 27.44 | other | yes | **yes** 27.41 / 0.21 | 0.47 | uncertain | not counted: right, but leaked | not (`uncertain_overlap`) |
| 28.10 | person | yes | no | 0.47 | uncertain | not counted: missed (said so) | not (`not_heard`) |

Non-overlapped words in a stretch (the `near` rule also flags them): 21.46 (person; room score 0.39, not counted before
either) -> uncertain; 24.16 (Scribe's invented word, nothing said) -> uncertain (not counted before either). Every
other word passed unchanged.

The same pattern in every run with this enrollment (d3, d4, v1, v2: the same words heard, word ends within 0.33 s,
scores within 0.07; PS4 and Nemotron are deterministic here, the hosted recogniser is not). With the enrollment taken from the same clip (`--voice-wav g.wav`, the owner-marked 1.5 s
at 11.02-12.52 s, which holds the 12.04 word: **chosen on this clip**, run d5g) the leaks stayed (12.63, 20.73,
27.41 heard), 19.87/19.97 were heard but too quiet to score (below -45 dBFS), all decisions uncertain.

Read without the voice score ("heard in the extracted sound" alone), PS4 would have counted 2 of the 3 overlapped
person words (19.74, 20.14) and 3 of the 3 other voices' words (12.64, 20.74, 27.44): the extraction does not remove
the other voices that speak next to or over the person here. It did remove the other person at 49-53 s (8-20 dB
quieter than the mixture, whole-clip run).

The TSE lane changed no count: it decided 8 words, all `uncertain` (not counted at once, and none of them was

(docs/measurements/catch-up-verification-2026-11-26.md) had 6 / 6 final; the difference to today's 4 / 4 is the L4

server (`ConnectionClosedOK`, code 1000) at the end of the input, so L1 lost its words from 51.84 s on (one other-person

probe, so concurrency alone is not the cause; not isolated.

## 5. Speed, workers, memory, latency

**PS4 alone** (this Mac's CPU, nothing else heavy, best of 3; RTF = compute / audio):

| threads | 3 s window | 5 s window | 8 s window |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | 0.65 | 0.72 | 0.68 |
| 4 (used in the runs) | 0.53 | 0.78 | 0.81 |
| 6 | 0.72 | 1.06 | 0.83 |
| 10 | 0.75 | 1.02 | 1.05 |

Peak RSS of that process 1.9 GB. Whole clip at once: RTF 0.60, RSS 5.6 GB (not how the lane runs). MPS: fails in the
publisher's `inference.py` (`hann_window(...).type(...)` with an MPS tensor type).

**The lane in the run** (v2, `workers` 1, with Nemotron-3 and the other modules on the same CPU):

| what | measured |
|---|---|
| stretches | 9 (21.8 s of the clip; 47.6 s of audio extracted with the 1.5 s margins) |
| extraction | 34.2 s for 47.6 s of extracted audio (RTF 0.72): **0.63 x real time** of the clip for one worker |
| hearing (a Scribe fork per stretch, concurrent with the next extraction) | 27.4 s in all; one stretch (41.3-44.0 s) got no answer and waited the 15 s timeout |
| voice scores | 1.5 s in all |
| catch-up | the live lane fell behind 4 times (17.9 s skipped), 4 TODOs done, 0 failed, **0 gaps**; TODO -> done p50 9.2 s, p95 34.2 s |
| memory | one PS4 worker, peak 1.7 GB (v2), 1.8-1.9 GB (v1, d5g) |
| latency, word end -> first count | words in a stretch 10.0-20.9 s (v2); without tse (v0) p50 4.0 s, p95 7.8 s; words not near a stretch unchanged (46.74 5.9 s, 47.82 4.8 s, 49.88 2.7 s); words in the same utterance as a stretch word wait with it (11.78: 10.8 s, 22.30: 18.3 s) |

**Two workers** (d4, `workers` 2, each fork its own PS4 process): no faster. The CPU is shared: extraction in the live
module took 27.2 s for 23.3 s of audio (RTF 1.17), TODO -> done p50 18.8 s, p95 30.4 s; 4 PS4 processes over the run,
at most 2 at once, 3.1 GB together.

So on this Mac one worker keeps up on average (0.63 x real time for this clip, whose stretches cover 40 % of it) and the
bursts (12-29 s, where most of the overlap is) are caught up by the Pipeline within 9-34 s; nothing is given up. More
workers need more cores (or a GPU), not more processes on the same cores.

Cost: each tse run sends about 48 s more audio to Scribe v3 Live (about $0.004 at the list price); all runs of this
note (d1-d5g, v0-v2 and a 4-session probe: about 20 minutes of Scribe audio, 3 x 54 s of gpt-live-transcribe, 12
Precision-3 windows) about $0.15 at list prices, under the $1 cap.

## 6. What this says

- The lane runs live inside the Pipeline, catches up without losing audio, and decides the overlapped words from the
  extracted voice, as the owner asked. On this clip it changed no count: PS4, conditioned on the person's voice (from
  another recording, or from 1.5 s of this clip), kept the other voices' words next to the person (12.64, 20.74,
  27.44) in the extracted sound, and the voice score of the extracted sound (0.21-0.47) never reached the module
  threshold, so every decision was `uncertain` -- no word was counted wrongly, none was gained.
- The live detector (`near`, Nemotron-3 slots) covered 7 of the 8 overlapped words; it missed 12.04 (its slots start the
  stretch at 12.31 s). It also flags 40 % of the clip.
- Overlapped words are counted (or said uncertain) 10-21 s after they are said on this Mac, against about 4 s without
  the lane: PS4 is not causal and runs on whole stretches, and one worker is busy through the overlap bursts.
- Not established here: whether PS4 separates Korean conversational voices better on other clips (it was trained on
  Mandarin and English meetings), and whether a GPU worker (lower latency, `workers` > 1 for real) changes the
  decisions. Both need the owner's clip set, not this clip.
